# Before submission

- Agreed files are pushed. Complete the remaining team cross-review.
- Actual AWS training and tuning runs are saved for both datasets unless the lecturer confirms a different minimum.
- Release models and thresholds are frozen before final testing.
- Reserved final tests are scored once and their results are kept.
- Both API paths work at a public address.
- A GitHub-triggered model update reaches the hosted app.
- A teammate reproduces the release and checks restart instructions.
- Notebook contributions, AI use and oral explanations are reviewed.
- The repository link is submitted in Canvas before 15 October, 23:59.

Keep raw trip ZIPs, environments and credentials out of GitHub. Keep genuine failed findings and model limits visible.
